# Grid City Visual System V2

## Scope

This is the single visual and naming source for the 16, 24, 32 and 40 tile boards. Smaller boards reuse the same tile IDs, names and images from the 40 tile catalog. They do not receive alternate names or substitute artwork.

The complete set contains 40 tile illustrations:

- 22 color-group properties
- 4 MetroLink transit assets
- 2 civic utility assets
- 12 special city-operation tiles

## Global Art Direction

- Clean American cartoon board-game illustration.
- Fixed three-quarter isometric camera, viewed slightly from above.
- One centered, fully visible subject occupying roughly 68-72% of the square.
- Crisp dark outlines, broad readable shapes and restrained two-step cel shading.
- Flat `#080C0E` background with no scene, skyline, people, vehicles or loose props.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signs, frames, particles, fog, gradients or cast shadows.
- Source masters are 1024 x 1024. Device derivatives are 160 x 160.
- Every image must remain recognizable when viewed at 160 x 160 on the round display.

## District Progression

| Group | District | Accent | Architectural language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Forge Quarter | `#C97852` | Brick, copper and compact industrial conversions |
| B | Harbor Quarter | `#63C6E8` | Airy civic buildings, pale glass and curved waterfront roofs |
| C | Arts Quarter | `#D970AD` | Creative venues, conservatories and expressive art-deco curves |
| D | Civic Quarter | `#EE9B47` | Warm stone, public institutions and ordered monumental forms |
| E | Pulse Quarter | `#E85D63` | Digital commerce, media and rhythmic illuminated facades |
| F | Solar Quarter | `#E7C64B` | Research campuses, solar forms and bright technical metal |
| G | Canopy Quarter | `#54BB78` | Eco-residential forms, integrated planting and faceted towers |
| H | Meridian Quarter | `#5F80D8` | Premium city core, deep-blue glass and restrained gold detail |

MetroLink uses graphite, cool white and silver `#AAB8BD` with tiny cyan signals. Civic Utilities use teal `#50D1B1`. Special tiles use a neutral civic base and one semantic accent only.

## Naming Rules

- Names are short, natural American English and readable on a small display.
- Each property name describes a believable urban place, not a generic technology slogan.
- Names within a district share tone without repeating the district name.
- Transit names use geographic direction plus a consistent network term.
- Special tile names describe their rule function while remaining part of the Grid City fiction.

The machine-readable complete list is `Assets/GridCity/UnifiedV2/manifests/grid-city-visual-catalog-v2.json`.
